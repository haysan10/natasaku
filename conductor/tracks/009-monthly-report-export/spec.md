# Track 009 — Monthly Report Export Spec

## Goal

Implement Monthly Report screen and local PDF/CSV export.

## Requirements

1. Show total income.
2. Show total expense.
3. Show total saving.
4. Show remaining balance.
5. Show biggest category.
6. Show most expensive day.
7. Show most frugal day.
8. Show overbudget day count.
9. Show average daily spending.
10. Show recommendation.
11. Export CSV.
12. Export PDF.
13. Show Export Success screen.
14. Allow share/open file.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Report metrics are accurate.
- CSV is valid UTF-8.
- PDF is created locally.
- Export success shows filename.
- Share/open works.
- No internet required.

## Out of Scope

- Cloud export.
- Email sending.
- Google Drive upload.
